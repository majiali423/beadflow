# Codex Project Instructions

The only active product in this workspace is `beadflow/`, a Web/PWA for scanning
existing bead-pattern sheets, counting occupied cells by color cluster, mapping
clusters to verified MARD codes, and supporting inventory decisions.

The former Flutter photo-to-pattern generator has been removed. Do not recreate
its generated-pattern editor, size/color caps, physical-board workflow, or
Easy-to-build generation mode.

Before changing product code, read `beadflow/AGENTS.md` and
`beadflow/SPEC.md` completely. Their requirements supersede all historical
workspace context.
